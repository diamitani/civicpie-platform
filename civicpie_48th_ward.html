48th Ward

The 48th Ward encompasses the vibrant neighborhoods of Edgewater and Andersonville on Chicago's North Side. Known for its diversity, walkability, and active community engagement, the 48th Ward is home to over 55,000 residents and some of Chicago's most celebrated small businesses, cultural institutions, and public spaces.

The office of Alderwoman Leni Manaa-Hoppenworth. Handles constituent services, neighborhood issues, development review, zoning, and community events.

Chicago 311

City ServiceCity

Non-emergency city services including potholes, street lights, graffiti removal, tree trimming, abandoned vehicles, and more. Available 24/7.

Chicago 211

Social ServicesCity

Health and social services support. Connects residents to community-based organizations and government agencies for help with housing, food, mental health, substance use, and more.

Chicago Department of Public Health (CDPH)

HealthCity

Provides public health services, immunizations, health inspections, environmental health, and emergency preparedness for all Chicago residents.

Chicago Department of Transportation (CDOT)

InfrastructureCity

Manages the city's transportation infrastructure including streets, sidewalks, bridges, traffic signals, and bicycle facilities.

Edgewater Chamber of Commerce

BusinessLocal

Promotes and supports local businesses in the Edgewater neighborhood. Organizes events, networking, and advocacy for the business community.

Chicago Board of Elections

ElectionsCity

Administers elections in Chicago. Handles voter registration, polling places, early voting, and election results.

Illinois Secretary of State

State AgencyState

Handles driver's licenses, vehicle registration, organ donation, and the Illinois State Library. Key resource for voter ID.
